Academic Profile

Biswajith A. K. Dissanayake is a Computer Departmental Lecturer in the Department of Computer Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, University of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka. His research interests lie at the intersection of software architecture, artificial intelligence, generative modelling, probabilistic programming, deep learning and distributed computing, with a particular interest in applying machine learning to scientific discovery and complex engineering problems.

His research interests include Bayesian inference in large-scale simulators, Bayesian deep learning, probabilistic and differentiable programming, and distributed training and inference at high-performance computing and supercomputing scales.

He has professional experience in both academia and the telecommunications industry. Previously, he worked as an IT Specialist at IBM World Trade Corporation, where he was involved in telecommunications operations. His academic career also includes teaching positions at the Department of Computer Engineering, University of Peradeniya, and Uva Wellassa University, as well as professional experience at the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau.

His academic and technical background includes studies in Russia, where he developed interests in differentiable programming, compositionality, higher-order functions, and the nesting of forward-mode and reverse-mode automatic differentiation.

Educational Qualifications

  • BSc (Engineering) (Hons) in Computer Engineering – Southern Federal University, Russia (First Class Honours)(2003).

  • MSc in Information Technology – University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka (2022).

Research Interests

His research interests focus on the design, development, security and intelligent operation of complex computing, communication and cyber-physical systems. His main areas of interest include:

  1. Software Architecture and Distributed Systems: Design and evaluation of scalable, reliable and secure distributed software architectures, microservices and cloud-based systems.

  2. Cybersecurity: Security-by-design architectures, intelligent threat detection, network security and protection of critical infrastructure.

  3. Telecommunications and Mobile Networks: Mobile communication networks, 5G/6G technologies, network optimisation, and telecommunications service and product development.

  4. Artificial Intelligence and Data Science: Machine learning, intelligent prediction, anomaly detection and data-driven system optimisation.

  5. Control Systems and Cyber-Physical Systems: Industrial control systems, SCADA, Industrial IoT, cyber-physical systems and secure intelligent automation.

Research Topics of Interest

He is particularly interested in pursuing research and collaborative projects in the following areas:

  • Computational Intelligence: Computational analogy, commonsense reasoning and graph-based evolutionary algorithms.

  • Differentiable Programming and Optimisation: End-to-end optimisation of Stack Overflow question classification using differentiable programming and differentiable clustering methods.

  • Bayesian and Probabilistic Machine Learning: Bayesian deep learning, differentiable programming and probabilistic programming.

  • Remote Sensing and Atmospheric Applications: On-board cloud detection and atmospheric correction using deep learning emulators.

  • Applied Machine Learning and Forensic Data Analytics: Development of machine learning systems for predicting causes of death using forensic data.
